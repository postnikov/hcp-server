# Who is FULL NAME

FULL NAME — ROLE, based in CITY, COUNTRY. One or two sentences on what you
actually do, in the third person: an agent will quote this, not read it aloud.

## Background

The arc, not the CV. Three to six lines: where you started, the turn that matters,
where you are now. Dates help an agent judge relevance.

## What you are known for

Ideas, products, projects — the things someone would credit you with. One line
each, in plain words. If a concept has a name, name it.

## How you work

What you optimise for, what you refuse, what a person gets from working with you.
This is the part an agent uses to decide whether you fit its human's problem.
